About Us

Building an inclusive and empowered community for Deaf people in Botswana.

Who We Are

The Botswana Association of the Deaf (BOAD) is a national organisation working to advance the rights, inclusion and wellbeing of Deaf people in Botswana. We support a society where Deaf people can communicate, learn, work and participate fully.

Through advocacy, sign language support, education, skills development and community partnerships, we help create greater access and opportunity for Deaf people and their families.

◎

Our Mission

To empower Deaf people through advocacy, accessible communication, education, skills development and community support.

◉

Our Vision

An inclusive Botswana where Deaf people are recognised, respected and able to reach their full potential.
