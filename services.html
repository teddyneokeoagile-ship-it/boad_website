Our Services

Support, access and opportunities for Deaf people in Botswana.

BOAD works with Deaf people, families and communities to promote communication, inclusion and equal opportunities. Explore some of the support and services we provide.

Sign Language Support

Sign language learning and communication support to help people connect and participate.

Community Support

Peer support, outreach and community activities that encourage connection and belonging.

Education & Training

Skills development and learning opportunities that support personal and professional growth.

Accessibility Advocacy

Promoting accessible services, facilities and communication in the wider community.

Advocacy & Awareness

Raising awareness of Deaf rights and encouraging equal participation and inclusion.

Guidance & Referrals

Connecting individuals and families with relevant information, support and services.
